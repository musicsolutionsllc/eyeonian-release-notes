Release notes for EyeOnian™ by Music Solutions LLC, across its native applications and its web version.

## Version 1.4

All platforms

- General improvements to the core application engine, and to navigation when saving EyeOnotes.
- Updated privacy policy to describe how your EyeOnotes are stored.

## Version 1.3

All platforms - Full access is now a subscription. Choose monthly or annual in the app; the demo remains free. Manage or cancel your subscription in your app store's subscription settings.

## Version 1.2

All platforms - Updated privacy policy to describe how purchases are handled on each platform.

iPadOS and macOS - Purchase handling updated. If your purchase isn't recognized, use Restore Purchases, or contact support@eyeonian.com.

<!-- not-apple:start -->
Android and web - Documentation update only. No changes to features or to how the app works.

## Version 1.0

The first public release of EyeOnian™.

EyeOnian™ is an interactive tool for exploring and visualizing harmony and scales. Browse every modal group and mode in Library view, identify the scales that fit your chord selections in Search view, and save your work as EyeOnotes.

We'll note what's new here with each update.
